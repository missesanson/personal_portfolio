Services

Writing with a clear purpose.

A focused set of services for people who know what they want to say—or know they need help finding it.

01

Research-led writing

Articles & explainers

Clear, well-structured articles that turn a difficult subject into something an intelligent general reader can follow.

Possible deliverables

Blog posts, explainers, educational articles and thought-leadership drafts.

Best suited to

Educators, founders, student organisations and small teams with expertise to share.

02

Words with a job to do

Website copy

Homepage, about-page and service copy that explains what you offer without sounding inflated or generic.

Possible deliverables

Landing pages, service pages, brand messaging and copy rewrites.

Best suited to

Small businesses, creators, community projects and early-stage brands.

03

Your ideas, in your voice

Ghostwriting

Posts and articles shaped from your notes, interviews or rough drafts, with careful attention to tone and point of view.

Possible deliverables

LinkedIn posts, founder articles, speeches, newsletters and personal essays.

Best suited to

People with strong ideas but limited time to turn them into polished writing.

04

Stronger without losing you

Editing & rewriting

Structural and line editing that improves logic, rhythm and readability while preserving the writer's meaning.

Possible deliverables

Developmental edits, line edits, rewrites and before-and-after explanations.

Best suited to

Students, independent writers, founders and teams working from an existing draft.

Process

A simple path from brief to clean copy.

01

Define the reader

We agree on the audience, purpose, tone, length and deadline before writing begins.

02

Research and shape

I gather the necessary material, identify the central argument and build a clean structure.

03

Draft and revise

You receive a complete draft, followed by an agreed revision round focused on useful feedback.

04

Deliver clean copy

The final version is proofread and supplied in a practical format ready to publish or hand off.

A note on ghostwriting

Your name belongs on work that genuinely represents your thinking.

I can shape interviews, notes and rough ideas into a coherent piece, but I will not invent achievements, expertise or personal experiences for a client. Confidential work stays confidential unless permission to display it is given.
