How I approach the work

Good writing begins before the first sentence.

It begins with the reader, the purpose and the one idea that must survive the page.

01

Clarity before cleverness

The reader should understand the point before admiring the sentence.

02

Research with restraint

Evidence should strengthen the argument, not bury it under unnecessary detail.

03

Voice, not imitation

Ghostwriting should sound like the client on their clearest and most thoughtful day.

04

Revision is the work

The first draft discovers the idea. The later drafts make it worth reading.
